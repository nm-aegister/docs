title: "Dismiss all notifications of a service"
sidebarTitle: "Dismiss all of a service"
description: "Remove every notification of one service (`service`, required) from your own list, for example all Cloud Defender notifications. Other members of the organization still see them. The service is required so the whole bell is never cleared by mistake. Within 10 minutes you can bring them back with “Undo dismissal”."
openapi: put /api/v1/notifications/dismiss-all
