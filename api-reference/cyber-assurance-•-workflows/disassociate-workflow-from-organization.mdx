title: "Disassociate workflow from organization"
sidebarTitle: "Disassociate workflow"
description: "End a framework (workflow) for an organization, the reverse of associating it, like switching it off in the organization's Compliance tab. Its evaluations and evidence are hidden, not deleted: associating the framework again brings them back. To delete the evaluations for good, use “Delete workflow evaluations”."
openapi: delete /api/v1/v-ciso/organizations/{organizationid}/workflows/{workflowid}
