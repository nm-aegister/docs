### Cloud Defender
Aegister Cloud Defender protects websites and web applications exposed on the internet.

- Your site is served through Cloudflare and Google Cloud, with DDoS mitigation and caching
- Origin lockdown: traffic that does not come through the protection is refused
- Threat Intelligence, updated continuously, blocks addresses already seen attacking other targets
- Requests for sensitive files, path traversal and other attack URLs are blocked
- Sources that keep requesting attack URLs are banned automatically for 24 hours
- Your own lists for each site: banned and always-allowed addresses, restricted pages and a country rule
- SQL injection, cross-site scripting and scanners are detected and listed for you to review
- You can switch on blocking of SQL injection and XSS for each site
- Every block is visible in the Cyber Console

[Learn more about Aegister Cloud Defender](/documentation/en/cloud-defender/what-is-cloud-defender)
