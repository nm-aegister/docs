## Account and sign-in

### How do I create an account?
Go to [app.aegister.com](https://app.aegister.com/), click **Log in**, then **Register**. The full
steps are in [Cyber Console: getting started](/documentation/en/acc/how-get-account).

### I signed up but only see "Welcome!". What now?
Your account is not linked to an organization yet. Ask an Owner of your organization to invite you,
or write to [info@aegister.com](mailto:info@aegister.com). If your email domain matches an
organization already in the console, you join it as a Viewer when you sign in.

### Which sign-in methods are available?
Email and password, or your **GitHub**, **Microsoft** or **Google** account. Signing in with a
password requires an authenticator app: you set it up at your first login and enter a one-time code
each time. To reset your password, click **Forgot password?** on the sign-in page.

### How do I change the language?
Use the **EN / IT** selector in the top bar. The choice is saved in your browser, not in your
account.

## Organizations, roles and licences

### What can each role do?
- **Viewer**: read only. Dashboards, Cyber Posture, evaluations and incident lists, no changes.
- **Security Officer**: everything a Viewer can do, plus evaluate and add frameworks, handle
  incident notifications, edit the organization profile and remove users.
- **Owner**: everything a Security Officer can do, plus add users and change their roles, manage VPN
  profiles and request licences.

### How do I get a licence, or more VPN seats?
Licences are activated by Aegister. On a page your organization has no licence for, an Owner can
click **Acquire license** and then **Request licenses**: this opens an email to
[info@aegister.com](mailto:info@aegister.com).

### Where can I see the statistics of all my organizations?
The **Dashboard** lists your organizations under **Your organizations** and shows a section for each
service you hold a licence for. When you have more than one, choose what to show with the selector
in each section: by device for Threat Blocker, by application for Cloud Defender, by organization
for vCISO and Incident Notifications.

## Virtual CISO

### How do I evaluate a compliance framework?
Open **vCISO → Evaluations** and click **Evaluate** on the framework's row. Pick a category and
click **Evaluate** again: the questions appear one per step, each with its answer, a justification
and evidence files. Click **Submit evaluation** on the last step, or **Save all & submit** from any
earlier step.

### How do I add a compliance framework to my organization?
Open **vCISO → Evaluations** and click **New framework**. Choose the organization and the framework
(for example NIS 2 or ISO 27001) and click **Save**. The list shows the frameworks Aegister offers.

### Why don't I see the Evaluate or New framework buttons?
Both need the organization's vCISO licence and the Security Officer or Owner role. Viewers see the
evaluations without these buttons.

### Does the console send incident notifications to CSIRT Italia for me?
No. **vCISO → Incident Notifications** prepares each NIS 2 stage with its deadline: the early
warning within 24 hours, the notification within 72 hours and the final report within one month.
You file each stage on the ACN portal, then click **Confirm Submission** in the console. This needs
the NIS 2 Incident Notification licence and the Security Officer or Owner role.

## Perimeter protection

### How can I check an IP, URL, domain or file?
Open **Perimeter Protection → Threat Intelligence**. The tabs check an **IP Address** (IPv4), a
**URL**, a **Domain**, a **File Digest** (MD5, SHA1 or SHA256) or a **File**. A file is not uploaded:
your browser computes its SHA-256 hash and only the hash is checked. Each tab needs its own Threat
Intelligence licence.

### How do I check the status of my web application?
Open **Perimeter Protection → Cloud Defender** and click your application's row. The **Cloud
Defender Status** page opens; **Export** saves it as a PDF.

### How often is Cloud Defender data updated?
Every hour. The page does not refresh on its own: reload it to see the latest data. **Last Update**
shows when the data was last written. The status page covers the last 7 days by default.

### How often is Threat Blocker data updated?
The device sends its traffic to the console as it runs. Reload the page to see new data: **Last
Update** is the time of the newest event received. If the console receives nothing from the device
for two hours, its card turns red.

### Which devices does Aegister VPN support?
The app runs on iOS and Android. On Windows, macOS and Linux, import the profile's `.ovpn`
certificate into an OpenVPN client. An Owner creates a profile in **Secure VPN → Manage VPNs → New VPN** with the
user's email address. The user receives an email with the app links and installs the profile from
**My VPNs → Install certificate**. Each profile uses one seat of the VPN licence; deleting it frees
the seat.

## API

### How do I create an API token?
Click your avatar at the top right and choose **Profile**. In the **API tokens** card click **New
token**, enter a name and click **Generate**. Copy the token straight away: it is shown only once.
Tokens do not expire. See [Token](/api-reference/token) for details.

### Where can I find the API documentation?
In the [API Reference](/api-reference/authentication) tab of this site.

## Support

through **Acquire license**.
