## Frequently Asked Questions

### Where can I find the statistics for all my organizations?
The **Dashboard** lists the organizations you belong to under **Your organizations** and shows a card for each service.  
On the **vCISO** and **Incident Notifications** cards, use the organization selector to choose whose data you see.

### How can I manually evaluate a compliance framework?
Open **vCISO → Evaluations** in the left-hand menu.  
On the framework's row, click **Evaluate**, answer the questions, then click **Submit evaluation**.

### How do I add a compliance framework to my organization?
Open **vCISO → Evaluations** in the left-hand menu and click **New framework** (Owners and Security Officers only).  
Choose the organization and the framework (for example NIS 2 or ISO 27001), then save.

### How can I check for threats related to URLs, files, and IPs?
Open **Perimeter Protection → Threat Intelligence** in the left-hand menu.  
There you can look up an IP, URL, domain or file hash to spot anomalies and threats.

### How do I check the status of my web app?
Open **Perimeter Protection → Cloud Defender** and click the row of your domain to open its status page.

### How frequently is Cloud Defender data updated?
Cloud Defender data is refreshed **every 5 minutes**.

### How can I create an API token?
Click your name in the **upper right corner** and choose **Profile**. In the **API tokens** card, click **New token**, enter a name and click **Generate**.  
Copy the token right away: it is shown only once. See [Token](/api-reference/token) for details.

### Where can I find the API documentation?
See the [API Reference](/api-reference/authentication) tab of this site.
