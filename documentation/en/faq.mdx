## Account and sign-in

### How do I create an account?
Go to [app.aegister.com](https://app.aegister.com/), click **Log in**, then **Register**. The full
steps are in [Cyber Console: getting started](/documentation/en/acc/how-get-account).

### I signed up but only see "Welcome!". What now?
Your account is not linked to an organization yet. Ask an Owner of your organization to invite you,
or write to [info@aegister.com](mailto:info@aegister.com). If your email domain matches an
organization already in the console, you join it as a Viewer when you sign in.

### Which sign-in methods are available?
Email and password, or your **GitHub**, **Microsoft** or **Google** account. Signing in with a
password requires an authenticator app: you set it up at your first login and enter a one-time code
each time. With GitHub, Microsoft or Google, your provider's own sign-in security applies instead. To reset your password, click **Forgot password?** on the sign-in page.

### How do I change the language?
Use the **EN / IT** selector in the top bar. The choice is saved in your browser, not in your
account.

### How do I find a page, a task or a control quickly?
Press **Cmd+K** (Mac) or **Ctrl+K** (Windows, Linux), or click **Search** in the top bar. The search
finds tasks, controls, documents, these guides, the FAQ and the training videos; **Enter** opens the
result.

## Organizations, roles and licences

### What can each role do?
- **Viewer**: read only. Dashboards, Cyber Posture, evaluations and incident lists, no changes.
- **Security Officer**: everything a Viewer can do, plus evaluate and add frameworks, handle
  incident notifications, edit the organization profile and remove users.
- **Owner**: everything a Security Officer can do, plus add users and change their roles, manage VPN
  profiles and request licences.

### How do I get a licence, or more VPN seats?
Licences are activated by Aegister. An Owner opens **Organizations**, clicks the organization and,
on the **Licenses** tab, clicks **Acquire licenses**; on a page your organization has no licence
for, the button is **Acquire license**. Pick the products and click **Request licenses**: this opens
an email to [info@aegister.com](mailto:info@aegister.com).

### Where can I see the statistics of all my organizations?
The **Dashboard** shows one organization at a time: a status tile per service, **Needs your
attention** (the most urgent items first), the V-CISO compliance per framework and a card for each
service with its own **Open** link. If you belong to more than one organization, switch between them
with the organization selector at the top.

## Virtual CISO

### How do I evaluate a compliance framework?
Open **vCISO → Evaluations** and click **Evaluate** on the framework's card. Pick a category: its
controls appear one at a time, each with its level, a justification and evidence files. Move with
**Next** and **Previous** and click **Save step** to save the category. You can also open a control
from **Cyber Posture** and click **Evaluate** there.

### How do I add a compliance framework to my organization?
Open **vCISO → Evaluations** and click **New association**. In three steps choose the
**Organization**, the **Framework** (for example NIS 2 or ISO 27001; the ones it already has are
marked) and **Check the profile**. The list shows the frameworks Aegister offers.

### Why don't I see the Evaluate or New association buttons?
Both need the organization's vCISO licence and the Security Officer or Owner role. Viewers see the
evaluations without these buttons.

### Does the console send incident notifications to CSIRT Italia for me?
No. In **vCISO → Incident Notifications** click **New incident** and go through three steps:
**What happened** (optionally starting from a template), **Is it significant?** and **Notification**.
Aegister then drafts each NIS 2 filing with its deadline: the pre-notification within 24 hours, the
notification within 72 hours and the final report within a month. On the incident page,
**Transfer to ACN** explains step by step how a browser bookmark fills the ACN portal form for you;
it never presses the portal's buttons. You press **Invia** on the portal yourself, then mark the
filing as filed in the console. **How it works**, on the incident list, sums up the process. This
needs the NIS 2 Incident Notification licence and the Security Officer or Owner role.

## Perimeter protection

### How can I check an IP, URL, domain or file?
Open **Perimeter Protection → Threat Intelligence**. Paste an IPv4 address, a URL (with `http://`
or `https://`), a domain or a file digest (MD5, SHA-1 or SHA-256) and click **Check**, or drop a
file on the page. A file is not uploaded: your browser computes its SHA-256 fingerprint and only that
is checked. Each kind of lookup needs its own Threat Intelligence licence.

### How do I check the status of my web application?
Open **Perimeter Protection → Cloud Defender** and click your application's row. The **Cloud
Defender Status** page opens with the **Overview**, **Attackers** and **Threat log** tabs;
**Export** saves it as a PDF. See [reading your status page](/documentation/en/cloud-defender/reading-your-status-page).

### How often is Cloud Defender data updated?
Every hour. The page does not refresh on its own: reload it to see the latest data. The status
page covers the last 7 days by default; choose
**24h**, **30 days** or **12 months** at the top.

### Which devices does Aegister VPN support?
The app runs on iOS and Android. On Windows, macOS and Linux, import the profile's `.ovpn`
certificate into an OpenVPN client. An Owner creates a profile in **Secure VPN → Manage VPNs → New VPN profile** with the
user's email address. The user receives an email with the app links and installs the profile from
**My VPNs → Install certificate**. Each profile uses one seat of the VPN licence; deleting it frees
the seat.

## API

### How do I create an API token?
Click your avatar at the top right and choose **Profile**. In the **API tokens** card click **New
token**, enter a name and click **Generate**. The token appears above the list: click **Copy**
straight away, it is shown only once.
Tokens do not expire. See [Token](/api-reference/token) for details.

### Where can I find the API documentation?
In the [API Reference](/api-reference/authentication) tab of this site.

## Support

### Where are the guides and the training videos?
Click the **?** icon in the top bar to open **Help and support**. The **Help** tab links to the
guides for each service and to common tasks; the **Training** tab has short narrated videos on the
console, the Virtual CISO and the other services.

### How do I contact Aegister?
Write to [info@aegister.com](mailto:info@aegister.com). Licence requests go to the same address
through **Acquire license**.
