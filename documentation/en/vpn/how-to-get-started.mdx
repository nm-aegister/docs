This guide walks you through the steps to start using **Aegister VPN**. Follow them to complete activation and configuration.

---

## 1. Request the VPN License

- **User license:**  
  The VPN license has one seat per user. To request it, or more seats, an Owner opens **Organizations**,
  clicks the organization and, on the **Licenses** tab, clicks **Acquire licenses**, picks the
  products and clicks **Request licenses**, which opens an email to Aegister.
  Aegister activates the license for your organization.

---

## 2. Organizational Profile

- **New customers:**  
  If you are a new customer, provide the information to create your organizational profile:
  - Company name and industry.
  - Names and email addresses of technical and administrative contacts.
  - Any other information the service activation requires.

---

## 3. Registering Users for Aegister VPN

After Aegister activates the license and you complete your organizational profile, register users:

1. **Open the management page:**  
   In the Cyber Console, open **Secure VPN → Manage VPNs** ([direct link](https://app.aegister.com/secure-vpn-management/)). Only organization Owners see this page.

2. **Add the user's email:**  
   Click **New VPN profile**, choose the **Organization**, enter the **Email address** of the employee who will use the VPN and click **Create profile**. Each profile uses one seat of the VPN license; the page shows how many seats are used.

3. **Activate the VPN on the device:**  
   After you add the emails, each user receives an email with instructions to connect to the VPN. In the mobile app, sign in with SSO or email and password; on other devices (Windows, Mac, or Linux), download the certificate and install it.

---

## Conclusion

Once you complete these steps, your organization is ready to use **Aegister VPN**. If you run into trouble or have questions, contact Aegister Support.

Aegister VPN gives you secure connections, protects your data privacy, and strengthens your corporate network.
