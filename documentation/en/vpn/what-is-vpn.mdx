## Key Features

- **Secure browsing:**  
  Encrypts your internet traffic and protects your data as you browse.

- **Privacy protection:**  
  Masks your IP address and location for anonymous, secure browsing.

- **Off-network security:**  
  Cuts the risk of interception on public Wi-Fi and keeps no activity logs.

## Benefits of Aegister VPN

- **Strong protection:**  
  All data travels through a secure tunnel, lowering the risk of cyberattacks and eavesdropping.

- **Reliable connections:**  
  Holds a stable, secure connection in the office or while you work remotely.

- **Easy to use:**  
  The app gives you strong protection without extra complexity.

## Download Aegister VPN

Protect your business and employees with our VPN.  
Available on:

- [Apple App Store](https://apps.apple.com/app/aegister-vpn/id6736686348)
- [Google Play Store](https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=com.Aegister.AegisterVPN)

On Windows, macOS and Linux, download your certificate (an `.ovpn` file) from **My VPNs → Install certificate** on the [Aegister Cyber Console](https://app.aegister.com/secure-vpn/) and import it into an OpenVPN client.
