This guide explains how to start using the services in the Aegister Virtual CISO plan. It covers how to create or access your account, verify your license, and navigate the **vCISO** section of the side menu.

## 1. Create or Access Your Account

To get started:

- **Create an account**: If you don't have one, follow the [account creation and first login](/documentation/en/acc/how-get-account) guide.
- **Log in**: If you already have an account, sign in at [app.aegister.com](https://app.aegister.com).

## 2. Verify Your Virtual CISO License

After logging in, check that the Virtual CISO license is active for your [Organization](https://app.aegister.com/organizations/). You need an active license to access the Virtual CISO services.  
<img className="block dark:hidden" src="/images/console/organizations-light.webp" alt="Organizations" />
<img className="hidden dark:block" src="/images/console/organizations-dark.webp" alt="Organizations" />  
To obtain a license, contact us.  

## 3. Navigating the vCISO Section

Once your license is confirmed, open **vCISO** in the side menu. It offers two main pages:
- **Cyber Posture**: shows the current status of your security policy implementation and compliance standards. View it [here](https://app.aegister.com/V-Ciso/).  
  <img className="block dark:hidden" src="/images/console/cyber-posture-light.webp" alt="Cyber Posture" />
  <img className="hidden dark:block" src="/images/console/cyber-posture-dark.webp" alt="Cyber Posture" />
- **Evaluations**: lets you add new security frameworks and complete the required tasks. Open it [here](https://app.aegister.com/V-Ciso/evaluations/).  
  <img className="block dark:hidden" src="/images/console/evaluations-light.webp" alt="Evaluations" />
  <img className="hidden dark:block" src="/images/console/evaluations-dark.webp" alt="Evaluations" />

## 4. Structure of the vCISO Section

The **vCISO** section has two main pages:

### 4.1 Cyber Posture
- **Audience**: all users within an organization.
- **Functionality**: shows the implementation status of the security activities defined in the frameworks associated with your organization.

### 4.2 Evaluations
- **Audience**: security officers and owners.
- **Functionality**:
  - Shows the security frameworks that Aegister has added to your organization.
  - Lets you add new security frameworks yourself with **New framework** (Aegister tailors them to your needs).  
    <img className="block dark:hidden" src="/images/console/new-framework-light.webp" alt="New framework" />
    <img className="hidden dark:block" src="/images/console/new-framework-dark.webp" alt="New framework" />
  - Starts the required remediation tasks.

## 5. Task Management

Tasks fall into two types:

- **Automated**: scheduled scripts defined in the security framework that run periodically, with a log of past executions.
- **Self**: tasks that require manual work.

### Task Evaluation and Documentation

- **Evaluate**: on **vCISO → Evaluations**, click **Evaluate** on a framework's row. The framework opens
  as a list of categories (for NIS 2, categories such as Asset Management or Supply Chain Management).
  <img className="block dark:hidden" src="/images/console/framework-questions-light.webp" alt="Framework categories" />
  <img className="hidden dark:block" src="/images/console/framework-questions-dark.webp" alt="Framework categories" />
- **Answer the questions**: pick a category to answer its questions one at a time. For each question,
  choose the answer level, add a justification and upload evidence (PDF, Office documents or images),
  then submit with **Save all & submit**.
  <img className="block dark:hidden" src="/images/console/evaluate-light.webp" alt="Evaluate a question" />
  <img className="hidden dark:block" src="/images/console/evaluate-dark.webp" alt="Evaluate a question" />
- **Evaluation history**: on **Cyber Posture**, open a framework and click a control. Its
  **Evaluation History** lists every past evaluation with date, evaluator, answer, justification and
  attached files, so you can track your progress.
  <img className="block dark:hidden" src="/images/console/evaluation-history-light.webp" alt="Evaluation history" />
  <img className="hidden dark:block" src="/images/console/evaluation-history-dark.webp" alt="Evaluation history" />

These steps let you start using the Aegister Virtual CISO plan and manage your organization's security tasks.
