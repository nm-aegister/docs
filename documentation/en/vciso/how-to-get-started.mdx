This guide explains how to start using the services in the Aegister Virtual CISO plan. It covers how to create or access your account, verify your license, and navigate the **vCISO** section of the side menu.

## 1. Create or Access Your Account

To get started:

- **Create an account**: If you don't have one, follow the [account creation and first login](/documentation/en/acc/how-get-account) guide.
- **Log in**: If you already have an account, sign in at [app.aegister.com](https://app.aegister.com).

## 2. Verify Your Virtual CISO License

After logging in, check that the Virtual CISO license is active for your [Organization](https://app.aegister.com/organizations/): open **Organizations**, click your organization and look at the **Licenses** tab. You need an active license to access the Virtual CISO services.
<img className="block dark:hidden" src="/images/console/organizations-light.webp" alt="Organizations" />
<img className="hidden dark:block" src="/images/console/organizations-dark.webp" alt="Organizations" />
To request a license, an Owner clicks **Acquire licenses** on that tab, picks the products and clicks **Request licenses**: this opens an email to Aegister.

## 3. Navigating the vCISO Section

Once your license is confirmed, open **vCISO** in the side menu. It has three pages:
- **Cyber Posture**: shows where your organization stands on each framework. The **Summary** tab gathers posture, controls, the tasks due next and progress over time; the **Documents** and **Tasks** tabs follow the documents and the remediation tasks; each framework (for example **ISO 27001** or **NIS 2**) has its own tab with its categories and controls. Open it [here](https://app.aegister.com/V-Ciso/).
  <img className="block dark:hidden" src="/images/console/cyber-posture-light.webp" alt="Cyber Posture" />
  <img className="hidden dark:block" src="/images/console/cyber-posture-dark.webp" alt="Cyber Posture" />
- **Evaluations**: shows one card per framework associated with your organization, with its score, how many controls are answered and its trend. From here you evaluate a framework or associate a new one. Open it [here](https://app.aegister.com/V-Ciso/evaluations/).
  <img className="block dark:hidden" src="/images/console/evaluations-light.webp" alt="Evaluations" />
  <img className="hidden dark:block" src="/images/console/evaluations-dark.webp" alt="Evaluations" />
- **Incident Notifications**: prepares the NIS 2 notifications of an incident for the ACN portal. See [the FAQ](/documentation/en/faq).

## 4. Structure of the vCISO Section

### 4.1 Cyber Posture
- **Audience**: all users within an organization.
- **Functionality**: shows the implementation status of the controls, tasks and documents of the frameworks associated with your organization.

### 4.2 Evaluations
- **Audience**: security officers and owners.
- **Functionality**:
  - Shows the frameworks associated with your organization, one card each, with **Evaluate** and **Open dashboard**.
  - Associates a new framework with **New association**, in three steps: **Organization**, **Framework** (for example NIS 2 or ISO 27001; the ones already associated are marked) and **Check the profile**.
    <img className="block dark:hidden" src="/images/console/new-framework-light.webp" alt="New association" />
    <img className="hidden dark:block" src="/images/console/new-framework-dark.webp" alt="New association" />

## 5. Task Management

Tasks fall into two types:

- **Automated**: scheduled scripts defined in the security framework that run periodically, with a log of past executions.
- **Self**: tasks that require manual work.

### Evaluating a Framework

- **Evaluate**: on **vCISO → Evaluations**, click **Evaluate** on a framework's card. The framework
  opens as a list of steps, one per category (for NIS 2, categories such as Asset Management or
  Supply Chain Management), each with its score and how many controls are answered.
  <img className="block dark:hidden" src="/images/console/framework-questions-light.webp" alt="Framework categories" />
  <img className="hidden dark:block" src="/images/console/framework-questions-dark.webp" alt="Framework categories" />
- **Answer the controls**: pick a category to answer its controls one at a time. For each control,
  choose how far it is in place (from **Not Existent** to **Optimized**, or **Not Applicable**), write
  a justification and add evidence (PDF, Office documents or images, up to 10 MB). Move with
  **Next** and **Previous**, then click **Save step** to save the category.
  <img className="block dark:hidden" src="/images/console/evaluate-light.webp" alt="Evaluate a control" />
  <img className="hidden dark:block" src="/images/console/evaluate-dark.webp" alt="Evaluate a control" />
- **Evaluation history**: on **Cyber Posture**, open a framework's tab and click a control. The
  control opens with its status, score and last change. **How it got here** shows each level the
  control reached and when, with the number of evaluations. **Answer** shows who gave the current
  answer, when, the maturity and the justification; below are the description, the linked task and
  the evidence. **Evaluate** reopens the control to answer it again.
  <img className="block dark:hidden" src="/images/console/evaluation-history-light.webp" alt="Control history" />
  <img className="hidden dark:block" src="/images/console/evaluation-history-dark.webp" alt="Control history" />

These steps let you start using the Aegister Virtual CISO plan and manage your organization's security tasks.
