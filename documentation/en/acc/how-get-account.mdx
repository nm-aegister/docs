This guide shows how to create an account and access the Aegister Cyber Console (ACC) using either of the two registration methods: with Single Sign-On (SSO) or without it.

## Scenario A: User Without SSO

If you do not use a Single Sign-On system, follow these steps:

1. **Open the Cyber Console:**  
   Go to [app.aegister.com](https://app.aegister.com/) and click **Log in**.
   <img src="/images/console/home.webp" alt="Cyber Console home page" />

2. **Registration:**  
   On the **Sign in to Cyber Console** page, click **Register** next to *New user?* at the bottom.
   <img src="/images/console/login.webp" alt="Sign in to Cyber Console" />

3. **Filling Out the Form:**  
   Enter your first name, last name, email and a password (twice), tick the reCAPTCHA box and click
   **Register**. Your email address becomes your username.
   <img src="/images/console/register.webp" alt="Registration form" />

4. **Adding Organizational Details:**  
   After you register, the console opens **New Organization**. Click **Manual setup** and fill in
   your organization's details.
   <img className="block dark:hidden" src="/images/console/org-setup-en-light.webp" alt="New Organization" />
   <img className="hidden dark:block" src="/images/console/org-setup-en-dark.webp" alt="New Organization" />

5. **Review by the Aegister Team:**  
   The Aegister team reviews your account. If your organization is new, they add the licenses your company purchased and activate your account.

## Scenario B: User With SSO

If you use a Single Sign-On (SSO) system, follow these steps:

1. **Accessing the Platform:**  
   Visit [https://app.aegister.com/](https://app.aegister.com/).

2. **Selecting Your SSO Provider:**  
   Click **Log in**, then under *Or sign in with* choose your provider (GitHub, Microsoft or Google).

3. **Verifying Your Information:**  
   - If the Aegister team has already linked your email address to your organization, you gain access to all relevant information and services right away.
   - Otherwise, the Aegister team reviews your account and, if it is a new organization, adds the purchased licenses.

Follow these steps to register and access the Aegister Cyber Console quickly and securely.
