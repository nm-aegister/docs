**Aegister Cloud Defender** is a Web Application Firewall (WAF) that protects web applications against common threats, including the **Top 10 OWASP Risks** such as SQL Injection and Cross-Site Scripting (XSS), and against DDoS attacks.

The system analyzes HTTP/HTTPS traffic in real time and blocks anomalous or harmful behavior, using customizable rules that Threat Intelligence keeps up to date.

## Key Operational Modules

- **Intrusion Prevention Module:**  
  Blocks malicious requests immediately.
  
- **Score Engine:**  
  Ranks threats, reduces false positives, and tunes response actions.
  
- **Network Threats Retriever:**  
  Updates the rules, signatures, and databases that the other modules use.

## Distinctive Features

### Interactive Dashboard

The **Aegister Cyber Console** gives you a complete view of what is happening to your domain.

<img className="block dark:hidden" src="/images/console/cloud-defender-light.webp" alt="Cloud Defender status page" />
<img className="hidden dark:block" src="/images/console/cloud-defender-dark.webp" alt="Cloud Defender status page" />

- **Service state:** licence, active services, domain and endpoint reachability, and when they were
  last checked.
- **Figures for the period:** requests blocked, distinct sources and the volume of traffic handled,
  over a window you choose.
- **Activity over time:** blocks and traffic volume day by day, with a zoomable range.

**Attacker insights.** Every blocked address is enriched with Aegister Threat Intelligence:
reputation, country of origin and the network it belongs to.

![Attacker insights](/images/cloud-defender-attacker-insights.png)

- **Blocked-threat log:** every denied request with its time, source, request and the layer that
  stopped it, searchable and exportable as CSV.
- **A weekly PDF report** and **email alerts**, with recipients chosen person by person (coming
  soon).
