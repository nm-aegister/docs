**Aegister Cloud Defender** protects websites and web applications exposed on the internet. Your site
is served through Cloudflare and Google Cloud, and every request passes through several layers of
protection before it reaches your server.

## Protection layers

- **Origin lockdown:** Cloud Defender accepts traffic only from Cloudflare and refuses everything
  else, so nobody can bypass the protection by reaching it another way. Locking your own server to
  Cloud Defender, as the activation guide explains, closes the remaining direct path.
- **Threat Intelligence:** requests from addresses on Aegister's threat-intelligence list, sources
  already seen attacking other targets, are refused before they reach your site.
- **Attack URL rule** (**Known attacks** in the Cyber Console): judges only the address and the
  headers of a request: hunting for sensitive files such as `/.env` or `/.git`, path traversal,
  Log4Shell strings, malformed or abusive HTTP and scanning tools that declare themselves. It never
  reads form fields, JSON bodies or cookies, so what your users type is never judged by it. A source
  that keeps sending these requests is also banned from your site automatically for 24 hours.
- **Your own lists:** you ban addresses, keep a whitelist of addresses that are always allowed,
  open login and admin pages only to the addresses or countries you choose, and refuse visitors by
  country. Changes apply within about two minutes.
- **SQL injection and cross-site scripting:** every request is also checked against the OWASP Core
  Rule Set (SQL injection, cross-site scripting and other attack patterns). By default these checks
  detect and do not block: the matching requests are listed for you to review. An Owner or Security
  Officer can switch on blocking for each site, and matching requests are then refused.
- **Scanner detection:** request patterns typical of scanners are detected and listed for you to
  review; they are not blocked.
- **Health checks:** Cloud Defender checks that your site responds and that its traffic really
  passes through the protection, and shows the result on the status page.

Cloudflare in front of the site also provides DDoS mitigation and caching.

## Distinctive Features

### Interactive Dashboard

The **Aegister Cyber Console** gives you a complete view of what is happening to your domain.

<img className="block dark:hidden" src="/images/console/cloud-defender-light.webp" alt="Cloud Defender status page" />
<img className="hidden dark:block" src="/images/console/cloud-defender-dark.webp" alt="Cloud Defender status page" />

- **Service state:** protection status and a check for the domain, the endpoint and the proxy.
- **Figures for the period:** requests blocked, distinct sources and the volume of traffic handled,
  over a window you choose.
- **Where and when:** a map of the threats stopped before your site and a weekday-by-hour view of
  when they arrive.
- **Activity over time:** blocks and traffic volume day by day, with a zoomable range.

**Attackers.** Every blocked address is enriched with Aegister Threat Intelligence:
reputation, country of origin and the network it belongs to.

![Attackers](/images/cloud-defender-attacker-insights.png)

- **Blocked requests:** every denied request with its time, source, request and the layer that
  stopped it, filterable by layer and exportable.
- **Detections:** what Cloud Defender flagged without blocking it, for you to review: mark a source
  **Legit** when its requests are expected, or **Ban** it.
- **Protection:** what each layer did in the period, and your lists of banned addresses, always
  allowed addresses, restricted pages and countries. A **Ban** button sits in every table.
- **Notifications** in the Cyber Console and **email alerts** when a domain's protection goes down,
  a daily notification when there are detections to review, and a **weekly PDF report** for the
  people you choose (coming soon, see
  [Alerts and reports](/documentation/en/cloud-defender/alerts-and-reports)).
