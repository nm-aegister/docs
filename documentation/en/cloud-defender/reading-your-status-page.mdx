The status page answers separate questions, and they are deliberately not the same number. Choose
the window at the top (**24h**, **7 days**, **30 days**, **12 months**); the **Threat overview**
holds the key figures and the tabs below go into detail: **Overview**, **Attackers** and
**Threat log**.

## Threats blocked

Requests Cloud Defender **refused**, over the selected window. Every one of them was stopped before
it reached your application.

## Attackers

The **Attackers** tab lists the sources we can identify, ranked by how many of their requests were refused, enriched with
Aegister Threat Intelligence: reputation, country of origin and the network each address belongs to.

The **Threat log** tab lists every request Cloud Defender denied, with the layer that refused it,
plus the refusals by your own application. **Export** downloads it.

## Protection status

The **Protection** figure reads **Active** when the licence is live and the health checks pass. A domain reads **Not active** if
either is false, including when the licence has ended, whatever the health check says. We do not
describe a domain as protected when we are no longer contracted to protect it.
