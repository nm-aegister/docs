The status page answers separate questions, and they are deliberately not the same number. Choose
the window at the top (**24h**, **7 days**, **30 days**, **12 months**); the **Threat overview**
holds the key figures and the tabs below go into detail: **Overview**, **Attackers**, **Blocked
requests**, **Detections** and **Protection**.

## Threats blocked

Requests Cloud Defender **refused**, over the selected window. Every one of them was stopped before
it reached your application.

## Attackers

The **Attackers** tab lists the sources we can identify, ranked by how many of their requests were refused, enriched with
Aegister Threat Intelligence: reputation, country of origin and the network each address belongs to.

## Blocked requests

The **Blocked requests** tab lists every request Cloud Defender denied, with the layer that refused
it in the **Blocked by** column: for example **Threat intelligence**, **Known attacks**, **IPs you
banned**, **Auto-ban**, **Country rule**, **Restricted page** or **Signature rule**. It also lists the requests your own
application refused, as **Your site**. **Export** downloads it.

## Detections

The **Detections** tab lists what Cloud Defender recognised, grouped by source: SQL injection and
cross-site scripting matches and scanner behaviour. Most of it was blocked by another layer. A row
that was not blocked reached your site: it is an anomaly, waiting for your decision. When SQL
injection and XSS blocking is on for the site, those matches are refused and show as **Blocked**.

- The filters at the top are **To review** (not blocked and still without a decision), **Not
  blocked**, **Blocked** and **All**. The tab title shows how many rows are to review.
- **Legit** says those requests are expected: the same kind of request from that source is no
  longer counted or alerted. **Undo** takes it back.
- **Ban** adds the source to your banned addresses.
- Open a row for its details: the request, what matched and the source, with **Legit** and **Ban**.

Everyone who can open the organization sees this tab. The captured request content is shown only
to Owners and Security Officers.

## Protection

The **Protection** tab shows how the site is protected and what each protection did in the
selected window, from **Direct access closed**, **Threat intelligence**, **Known attacks** and
**SQL injection and XSS** to your own lists: **Banned IP addresses**, **Countries**, **Whitelist**
and **Restricted pages**. An Owner or Security Officer edits the lists here, and switches the
**SQL injection and XSS** row between **Detecting** and **Blocking**; the other roles see them.
After you remove an entry, unban an address or mark a source **Legit**, **Undo** appears for a few
seconds and puts it back.

## Protection status

The **Protection** figure reads **Active** when the licence is live and the health checks pass. A domain reads **Not active** if
either is false, including when the licence has ended, whatever the health check says. We do not
describe a domain as protected when we are no longer contracted to protect it.
