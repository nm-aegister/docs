---
title: "Cloud Defender: when a licence ends"
sidebarTitle: "When a licence ends"
description: "What happens to your domain and your data when a Cloud Defender licence expires."
---

**Protection stops. Your data stays.**

When a Cloud Defender licence expires, we stop protecting the domain, but the instance and everything
it recorded remain. You keep access to it.

- The domain still appears in your Cloud Defender list, with the licence shown as
  **Expired &lt;date&gt;** and the status as **Not active**.
- Its status page still opens, and the traffic history, attacker insights and detections are all
  still readable.
- Nothing can be reconfigured, and no new traffic is recorded, because the domain is no longer
  behind Cloud Defender.

To restart protection, renew the licence. The same instance and its history resume; nothing needs
to be set up again.

You are warned before this happens: 30 days before the expiry date, again the day before, and once
more on expiry. Those notices go to the organization's Owners and to any administrative point of
contact.
