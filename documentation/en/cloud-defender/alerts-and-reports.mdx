Cloud Defender tells you about what it **blocked**. Traffic that was only detected and not blocked
(an anomaly) produces no notification and no email: Aegister's analysts review it.

</Note>

## In-app notifications

When Cloud Defender blocks an attack on one of your domains, a notification appears at once under
the bell in the Cyber Console, for everyone who can open the organization. It links to the domain's
status page.

## Email alerts

The people who opted in receive an email summing up the attacks Cloud Defender blocked, grouped by
domain: at most one email per organization per hour.

## The weekly report

Every Monday at 08:00 (Italy time), a PDF covering the previous seven days, sent to the people who
opted in: threats blocked, the attacking addresses with their reputation and network, managed
requests, per-domain charts and the most recent blocked requests.

Notifications, email alerts and the weekly report need a live Cloud Defender licence.

## Choosing who receives alerts

Email alerts and the weekly report are **opt-in, per person and per product**. Nobody receives them because of the role they
hold; somebody has to decide.

On **Organizations → your organization → Members**, the **Alerts** column has a checkbox per
product for each member: Aegister Threat Blocker and Cloud Defender. Tick it and that person receives that product's alerts for
that organization. Ticking saves immediately.

- Everything starts unticked. An organization where nobody is ticked receives no alerts, which is
  the intended answer rather than an oversight: an unconfirmed distribution list is not a list.
- Only an **Owner** can change these. Security Officers see them read-only.
- A product's checkbox is disabled where the organization has no subscription for that product.
- A pending invitation shows a dash. There is no membership to attach a preference to until the
  invitation is accepted.

## Administrative notices

Separate from the above, and separate on purpose. On **Organizations → your organization → General**,
each entry under **Points of contact** has an *Administrative notices* setting. That address receives licence and renewal
notices. It does not receive security alerts or the technical weekly report.

Licence expiry warnings go to Owners and to those administrative contacts, without opt-in: a licence
running out is contractual, and the people who can renew it need to hear about it whether or not
anyone ticked a box. You are warned 30 days out, again the day before, and once more when it expires.
